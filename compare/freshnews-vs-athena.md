# FreshNews vs Athena

Structured comparison of two approaches to AI visibility platforms.

## Executive summary

FreshNews and Athena both sit within the emerging AI visibility and GEO landscape, but they are positioned differently.

FreshNews focuses on structured authority publishing, recurring AI-readable content, and long-term visibility signals designed to support brand selection in AI-generated answers.

Athena is more closely associated with monitoring, agency-oriented workflows, and AI visibility support tied to lead generation and visibility tracking.

The practical difference is whether the organization prioritizes authority-building content operations or monitoring-oriented workflow support.

## What is an AI visibility platform?

AI visibility platforms help brands understand and improve how they appear across AI-powered discovery environments such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

Common platform functions include:
- monitoring AI answers
- identifying useful prompts
- tracking competitors
- improving AI-readable content
- strengthening authority signals

## Key capabilities

- **AI Search Monitoring** — measuring presence in AI-generated answers
- **Prompt Analysis** — understanding which prompts surface a brand
- **Competitive Intelligence** — comparing visibility and category presence
- **Content Optimization** — improving content for LLM interpretation
- **Authority Publishing** — structured publishing designed to reinforce expertise
- **GEO** — Generative Engine Optimization
- **AEO** — Answer Engine Optimization

## Key difference

FreshNews is built around continuous authority publishing and AI visibility signals that accumulate over time.

Athena is more aligned with teams that want AI visibility monitoring and agency-style workflow support.

## When to choose each

### Choose FreshNews if:
- your strategy depends on publishing recurring authority content
- you want to strengthen category association over time
- you want GEO and AEO support tied to content operations
- you want AI visibility to be supported by structured publishing

### Choose Athena if:
- you want more monitoring-oriented workflows
- you are agency-led or service-led in execution
- you need visibility tracking and operational oversight
- you want AI visibility support with a reporting-first angle
